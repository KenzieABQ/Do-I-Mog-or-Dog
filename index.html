Step 01 · Pre-Capture

Do I Mog or Dog: Calibration Protocol

Lens distortion, lighting and head posture swing these measurements by whole tiers. Confirm each control before capture.

01

Focal Length Calibration

6.5 ft+2.9% nasal inflation
  • Shoot from ≥ 6 ft (2 m) away using 2× / 3× optical zoom.
  • Selfie-distance wide-angle lenses inflate midface length and nose size and hide the ears.
02

Lighting

✓ Even, shadow-less: true geometry visible
  • Flat, shadow-less frontal light (window or ring light in front).
  • Top-down light fakes brow ridge projection; side light distorts symmetry.
03

Neutral Posture & Expression

  • ◉ Strictly neutral face
  • ▤ Teeth closed in occlusion
  • ◡ Soft lip closure, no strain
  • ✕ No smiling
  • ◎ No squinting, eyes relaxed
  • ↧ Shoulders down, no chin tuck
  • Smiling raises the cheilion and compresses the eye area; squinting fakes positive tilt.
04

Frankfurt Horizontal Plane

+6°Head pitched up: tilt down
  • The line from the infraorbital rim (orbitale) to the top of the ear canal (porion) must be 0°, parallel to the ground.
  • Chin up flattens the jaw and shrinks the gonial angle; chin down does the reverse.
